INSTRUCTOR REFERENCE

What the model explains.

Course content comes from your CSMART Advanced Ship Handling and Manoeuvre course with podded propulsion (2023), especially pages 3–8 and 14–19. The notes reference ABB recommendations. This is not an ABB product or endorsement.

Motion uses the supplied Harbour Master v86 Queen Victoria 3-DOF model unchanged: surge, sway, yaw, added mass, hull resistance, pod interactions, finite RPM ramp and 2.5°/s normal steering. No scripted ship displacement is used. The application runs fixed 1/30-second physics steps.

The 3D ship is a procedural, proportioned cruise-ship illustration, not a surveyed Queen Victoria asset. Hull length and beam, pod lever positions, heading, delivered azimuth and RPM follow the model. Deck details, propeller geometry, sea surface and discharge particles are illustrative. Flow particles are not CFD, and no heeling, roll or wave-driven vessel motion is modelled.

Thrust arrows and the force strip show propeller thrust only; they exclude housing, hull, wind and other forces. The hull responds to the engine’s total force balance. A combined arrow at the vessel centre shows the equivalent translational force; the turning moment is displayed separately. Arrow length is scaled consistently within a view; it is not speed or acceleration.

Angles are thrust directions: 000° ahead, 090° starboard, 180° astern, 270° port. IN/OUT labels translate the course convention for each side; controls do not reproduce an ABB lever. Positive RPM orders below 25 command zero thrust in this vessel model, with a ramp-down delay. Harbour Master limits delivered azimuth commands at water speeds above 8 kn. Standard classroom scenarios start below that limit.

Turning and stopping comparisons run identical initial conditions and elapsed time through the model. They are predictions, not sea-trial results or manoeuvring-booklet values. Some coefficients have reference-based calibration; pod interactions, stopping and environmental effects remain incompletely validated. No claim of certified simulator fidelity or complete course coverage is made.

Everything needed at runtime is embedded in the downloadable HTML. Harbour Master attribution · Three.js MIT licence.
